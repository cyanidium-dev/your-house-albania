# Lead landings from Ahrefs demand (2026-10-11)

Which low- and mid-volume searches a Domlivo landing can win, what each landing
contains, and in what order to build them. Input: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer
(Matching terms, 92 seeds × 8 markets, pulled 2026-10-11 through the app's
internal API: 3,505 keywords, 486 commercial/transactional with ≥ 10 searches
a month after removing rentals, holidays, weather, news), Google SERPs checked
the same day in each market (`pws=0`, local `gl`/`hl`), our inventory in
Sanity on 2026-10-11, and the earlier research in `keyword-research.md` and
`INVESTMENT-LANDINGS-PLAN-2026-10-08.md`.

Volumes are Ahrefs monthly estimates for one country (Google). KD is Ahrefs
keyword difficulty (0–100); "–" means Ahrefs has no SERP for it, which in
practice means a thin SERP.

## 1. What the data says

1. **Foreign buyers search the country and the sea, not Durrës.** In Poland,
   Germany, the UK/US, Italy and Ukraine the volume sits on "property in
   Albania by the sea" phrasing. Durrës-named foreign queries are 10–30 a
   month (`durres property for sale` 20 GB, `durres wohnung kaufen` 30 DE,
   `case in vendita a durazzo albania` 30 IT). We have no page in those
   languages that answers the country-level query; every result in those
   SERPs is a portal category page.
2. **Difficulty is close to zero.** Almost every foreign cluster head has
   KD 0–3 (`nieruchomości albania` 300/mo KD 2, `albania real estate` 500/mo
   US KD 0, `property for sale albania` 200/mo GB KD 1). The exceptions are
   German "haus kaufen" variants (KD 35–37) and Albanian Tirana (KD 38).
3. **Budget is its own intent.** `immobilien albanien bis 50.000 euro` (100
   DE), `albanien haus kaufen günstig` (150 DE), `купить квартиру в албании
   недорого` (150 UA), `cheap (rural) property for sale in albania` (50+30),
   `property for sale in albania under 20k`, and in Albanian `okazion`
   ("bargain") on Durrës: `apartamente ne shitje durres okazion` 200,
   `shtepi ne shitje durres okazion` 150, `…plazh okazion` 90. The EN and IT
   SERPs both rank a dedicated "Albania under €50k" page (holprop).
4. **Albanian demand is local and by place.** Durrës: `shtepi ne shitje
   durres` 450, `apartamente ne shitje durres` 300 (both already served by
   our city listing page), plus districts the registry already covers. New
   here: `shtepi private ne shitje durres` 200 (houses), `garsoniere ne shitje
   durres` 70 (+ plazh 50, golem 50), `apartamente ne shitje shengjin me
   pamje nga deti` 60. Tirana (2,400, KD 38) and Vlorë (200, plus lungomare
   150) are large but we hold 15 and 10 listings there.
5. **Ukrainian searchers write Russian.** `купить квартиру в албании` 200,
   `недвижимость в албании для украинцев` 150, `…недорого` 150 come from
   Ukraine; Russia adds `купить недвижимость в албании` 80. Ukrainian-language
   variants are ~100 each.

## 3. What each landing contains (conversion layout)

Same skeleton for all ten, in the landing's own language only (`locales`
set to that one locale; the copy is written in that language, not
translated):

1. **Hero** — H1 = the cluster head in natural phrasing; one line with the
   live count, the lowest price and the median €/m² of the landing's filter;
   two CTAs: "Get a selection in WhatsApp/Telegram" (messengers) and
   "Leave your budget" (scrolls to the form).
2. **Inventory band** (auto) — count, from-price, median €/m², share within
   300 m of the sea, new builds; computed at render from the same filter.
3. **Listing feed** — 12 cards from the catalogue filter (sea / budget /
   houses), sorted by €/m², with "See all N" to the catalogue URL carrying
   the same filter. A second feed where the cluster asks for it (L1/L2/L4:
   "under €70k"; L6: "with a sea view"; L9: by district).
4. **Prices by city / district** — `zonePriceTableAutoSection` (live medians).
5. **What it costs to buy** — `purchaseCostCalcSection` with the foreigner
   costs (notary, ASHK, lawyer, translation, agency 1%).
6. **What it earns** — `roiCalcSection` with the Durrës presets of the
   investment plan (§3: STR most-likely net 5.8% self-managed, LTR 2.0%),
   labelled as estimates with sources.
7. **Lead form** — new `leadFormSection`: name, phone/WhatsApp, budget,
   message; posts to `/api/contact-agent` with placement `landing` and the
   landing slug; the Telegram message names the landing.
8. **FAQ** — 6–8 questions taken from the cluster's question searches: can
   a foreigner buy (yes, same terms), total cost on top of the price, taxes
   (15% rental income, 0.05% building tax), residence permit (no golden
   visa), remote purchase with a power of attorney, how we check the
   developer, why "by the sea" prices differ by line.
9. **Short intro text** (`seoTextSection`, 150–250 words) — unique per
   landing; what the reader gets on this page, number-first.

SEO: title ≤ 60 characters with the head term and a number ("ponad 600
ofert", "ab 15.000 €"), meta description with count + from-price + CTA;
`FAQPage` + `ItemList` (the feed) + `BreadcrumbList`; self-canonical;
listed in `sitemap-static` for its one locale only; IndexNow + GSC request on
publish.

## 4. What the site needs first

- `leadFormSection` (Sanity object + renderer around `QuickLeadForm`), lead
  placement `landing`, landing slug in the lead.
- `propertyCarouselSection` auto mode: add `minPrice`, `maxPrice`,
  `nearSea`, several cities, `sort`, and a "See all" link to the catalogue
  with the same query.
- `inventorySummarySection` (auto): count / from-price / median €/m² /
  near-sea share for a filter, in the page locale.
- Sitemap: a landing restricted with `locales` must be emitted only for
  those locales (today `sitemap-static` lists every landing in every locale
  and would publish 404s).
- Data: `seaDistanceMeters` was set on 84 of 720 sale listings, so "near the
  sea" matched ~97. It is being computed for every listing from the
  OpenStreetMap shoreline (2026-10-11) so the sea feeds and the band are
  real.

## 5. Order and measurement

Wave 1 (biggest clusters, lowest KD): **L1 pl, L2 de, L4 en, L6 ru**.
Wave 2: L8 it, L7 uk, L9 sq okazion. Wave 3: budget pages L3 de, L5 en,
and L10 sq houses (after checking Durrës house stock).

Measure per landing in GSC (impressions, position for the cluster queries,
clicks), Clarity (scroll to the feed, clicks on cards, form views), and
leads by `placement = landing` + slug. Review 2026-11-12 together with the
registry experiments: a landing with impressions but no clicks gets a title
pass; one with no impressions after 6 weeks gets merged into its parent.
