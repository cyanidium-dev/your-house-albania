# E003 - Listing page on phones: photos first or figures first

Status: running from 2026-10-24 (proposed 2026-10-21) · sample check **2026-11-07** · review **2026-11-21** · rules of [ADR 007](../seo/decisions/007-experiments.md)

## Context

Since PR #93 a listing page opens on the figures: title, place links, the facts row (rooms, bedrooms, bathrooms, area, distance to the sea), the price and the agent, with the gallery below. On a 390 px phone the first photo starts at about 630 px, so the first screen shows a strip of it. Clarity, 30 days, listing pages on phones (855 page views, 4 705 taps): the gallery controls take 34 % of all taps (the next-photo arrow alone 28 %), more than anything else on the page, and 9.3 % of listing sessions end in a quick back. The portals visitors compare Domlivo with (Idealista, Immobiliare, Zillow) open a listing on the photo.

Both orders have a case: the figures answer "what, where, how much" at once (the price is also in the sticky bar), the photos are what visitors actually touch. Which one leads to more conversations is an empirical question.

## Hypothesis

Putting the gallery right under the breadcrumb on phones increases the share of listing viewers who start a conversation, because the visitor sees the flat at once, while the price and the contact buttons stay on screen in the sticky bar.

## Metrics

- **Primary - conversation rate**: share of exposed visitors with `messenger_open`, `property_inquiry` or `contact_form`.
- **Secondary - intent rate**: `click_whatsapp` or `click_telegram`; and a second listing opened (≥ 2 `property_view`).
- **Guardrail - inquiry rate** (`property_inquiry`): must not fall by more than 20 % relative.

## Sample size

At a 3 % conversation rate (about 25 lead sessions on 858 listing sessions in Clarity's last 30 days), a 30 % relative lift needs about 6 000 exposed visitors per variant; four weeks of consented phone traffic gives a few hundred. A win is possible only for a large effect; the secondary metrics will move first. Write the baseline and weekly exposures on 2026-11-04.

## Decision at review (2026-11-21)

1. **`photo-first` wins** (win probability ≥ 90 % on the primary metric, guardrail holds): make it the phone order, delete the flag.
2. **`control` wins**, or the guardrail fails: remove the variant classes, delete the flag.
3. **No clear winner**: keep the control, delete the flag, record the reading. If only the second-listing rate rose, use that for the similar-listings block, not the order.
